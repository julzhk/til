This new phase of Generative-AI calls for a new vocabulary of experiences. I'll add the one's I've found & add the new experience that need a name. 

* [AI Slop](https://simonwillison.net/2024/May/8/slop/) : That's Simon Willison's coining. Amazing how the term's taken-off: we're all living it.. 
* [AI Mania](https://ludic.mataroa.blog/blog/ai-mania-is-eviscerating-global-decision-making/): 'This time it's different' DHH and Yegge, we're looking at you..
* [AI Psychosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI-induced_psychosis?utm_source=substack&utm_medium=email) 
* [Deep Blue Feeling](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/15/deep-blue/)
* [Roko's Basilisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roko%27s_basilisk): AI will take over the world and know everything ever written, so you better say nice things about our future AI overlords now.  I, for one, welcome them.
* code factory: So so ([50?](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/steve-yegge-on-ai-agents-and-the)) many agents, all coordinated with their own jobs. Either an AI version of a victorian child-threashing sweatshop of a modern Lean process masterclass in organization. 
* [Slop Grenade](https://fortune.com/2026/09/17/shopify-tobias-lutke-ai-slop-grenades/): Submitting a PR without any consideration of code review or consequences. 2000 files changed in one PR? YOLO!
* [The Dracula effect](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/steve-yegge-on-ai-agents-and-the): using AI is draining. (Employers shouldn’t reasonably expect more than three hours per day of 
  AI-augmented work from engineers? Yeah that's going to happen.)
