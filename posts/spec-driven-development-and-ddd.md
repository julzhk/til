TIL the cross-over between AI assisted development and DDD
---

DDD is a great tool for approaching a technical problem: useful concepts and useful methodology to write the code.

Of course these days we can (debatably!) defer that code-creation to AI. Perhaps we can concentrate on the design and architecture and let the AI handle the implementation details? 
 
Turns out this isn't an original idea. Various forms of ['spec-driven development' are available.](https://martinfowler.com/articles/exploring-gen-ai/sdd-3-tools.html): but this smart reviewer is 
luke-warm on the whole idea.

This company has several interesting blog posts: [Code centric: Domain-Story to Prototype](https://www.codecentric.de/en/knowledge-hub/blog/from-domain-story-to-prototype) & [Five principles post](https://www.codecentric.de/en/knowledge-hub/blog/five-principles-for-ai-assisted-ddd-keeping-the-human-at-the-center) 
have a lot of detail. Reference list is worth a read in itself. Some I recognize (and recommend), and the academic literature is unknown to me, so that's a good opportunity to dive deeper!
