---

Turns out that if you ask nicely and go into detail on the exact era/style and feeling, that's what you get.


I just asked chatGPT to create an image:

```
create a modernist / Bauhaus-influenced graphic style, 
with a bit of contemporary “flat illustration” layered on top. 

More specifically: Bauhaus / Modernist Poster rooted in the Bauhaus tradition (1920s–30s) 

Strong emphasis on: function over decoration. 
Asymmetrical layout with clear hierarchy Bold, simple sans-serif typography. 
Limited, high-contrast colour palette .
Geometric Minimalism: Built from basic shapes (circles, semicircles, rectangles) 
Shapes are abstracted into almost symbolic forms.
Very little texture or shading — mostly flat colour. 
Swiss Style (International Typographic Style) influence. 
Clean grid-based alignment (even if loosely applied). 
Information-first layout & a clear, legible structure. 

In short a : “Bauhaus-inspired geometric minimalist poster” 

The topic and heading should be '<Your content here>

```
This comes out quite tastefully imho.
