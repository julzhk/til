## This site

A personal site and a running notebook. I am (by far) it's main reader, no doubt.  

The site really grew out of the [blogging](blog/index.html) as a "Today I Learned" process: started enjoying producing short write-ups of things I've picked up day-to-day. I've found framing a blog 
around 
'TIL' 
makes 
posting a lot easier than 
other 
ways of blogging: and 
I'm always learning new things. It works for me. And the rest of the site grew from there. 

## The blogging platform

I've found a roll-your-own approach works for me: The site's a folder for markdown files that are converted and formatted by Jinja templates. Jinja has a nice 'macros' feature which works 
neatly 
for UX 
components. Deployed by calling an 'invoke' task and it's simple, extensible and low-friction.
